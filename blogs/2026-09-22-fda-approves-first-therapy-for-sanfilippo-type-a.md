On 17 September 2026, the FDA granted standard full approval to FAYUVI (rebisufligene etisparvovec-hopf), a single-dose intravenous AAV9 gene therapy from Ultragenyx, for pediatric patients with MPS IIIA, or Sanfilippo syndrome Type A. It is the first approved treatment for a fatal, ultra-rare neurodegenerative disease that previously had none. Sanfilippo Type A is caused by deficiency of the sulfamidase (SGSH) enzyme , which drives heparan sulfate accumulation and progressive damage to the central nervous system from early childhood.
