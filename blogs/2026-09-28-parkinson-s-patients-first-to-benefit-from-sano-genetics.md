Sano Genetics and Patients Know Best (PKB) are excited to announce a new partnership to streamline and accelerate clinical trial recruitment processes, launching their first collaborative project to support a Parkinson’s disease trial. By leveraging coded GP data alongside targeted digital banner functionality, the initiative enables patients who match trial criteria to securely view trial information in the PKB platform and opt in, bringing a more democratic, patient-led approach to medical research.
